Gardens and Outdoors — 6 Verified Options

Portland blooms harder than any city in the Northwest right now. These are all worth the trip.

Crystal Springs Rhododendron Garden

Nine acres at peak bloom in SE Portland's Eastmoreland neighborhood — rhododendrons and azaleas reflecting off the lake. Free in May.

📅 Open Mothers Day weekend
📍 5801 SE 28th Ave, Portland | 💰 Free in April and May

Crystal Springs sits in one of Portland's most settled, tree-lined neighborhoods. Walk in when the rhododendrons are at peak and it is one of the most beautiful places in the city.

Learn More

Leach Botanical Garden Mothers Day Celebration

Spring blooms, live Celtic harp music, and refreshments in a historic manor house — one of the least-known great things happening this Sunday.

📅 Sunday May 10
📍 6704 SE 122nd Ave, Portland (Lents neighborhood)

Out in Lents — a neighborhood most visitors never venture to. The manor house setting puts this a level above a standard garden visit. Worth building a whole day around.

Learn More

Portland Japanese Garden

Twelve woodland acres in the West Hills — one of the most authentic Japanese gardens outside Japan. Book tickets ahead.

📅 Open Mothers Day weekend
📍 611 SW Kingston Ave, Portland | 💰 Ticketed — book in advance

Portland is lucky to have something this caliber. It is about the whole environment, not just the flowers. One of my first recommendations to anyone visiting the city.

Learn More

Lan Su Chinese Garden Mothers Day Activities

Corsage-making, henna painting, a peony photo op, and a tea tasting with a tea ambassador — a completely different pace from the rest of Portland.

📅 Sunday May 10, 9 AM onward
📍 239 NW Everett St, Portland Pearl District | 💰 Ticketed

People walk past the walls of Lan Su in the Pearl all the time and have no idea what's inside. One of the most undervisited spots in a neighborhood that gets a lot of foot traffic.

Learn More

Adelman Peony Gardens Open House

Production fields near Salem open for walking — free admission, cut flowers and potted peonies for sale. The trunk-full-of-plants version of Mothers Day.

📅 Open daily April 25 through June 15, 9 AM to 6 PM
📍 Near Salem, Oregon | 💰 Free admission

A 45-minute drive and worth every minute. Walking through peony fields and leaving with an armful for under $20 is not something most people know is possible.

Learn More

Hulda Klager Lilac Days Woodland WA

100+ lilac varieties at peak bloom, plant sale, museum, and house tours — 40 minutes north of Portland and criminally undervisited.

📅 All weekend through May 10
📍 115 S Pekin Rd, Woodland, WA | 💰 $5

Come for the blooms, leave with enough plants to fill a garden. Lilac season is the only time this place gets real foot traffic and it's still not crowded enough.

Joe's Hidden Picks

The places I recommend when someone asks what to do that they have never done before. Not on most lists. All worth it.

The Belmont Goats

A volunteer-run community goat herd living on a vacant lot in SE Portland. Free. Takes 20 minutes. Impossible not to love.

📍 SE Belmont neighborhood, Portland
💰 Free, donations welcome | Check belmontgoats.com for hours

A small herd of goats on a vacant lot managed by Portland volunteers who decided this was a good use of urban space — and they were right. Works every time as a Portland introduction.

Learn More

Pittock Mansion — Go at Sunrise

Portland's most underused spectacular view. Arrive before 9 AM on a clear morning and you get Mt. Hood, Mt. St. Helens, and the whole city almost to yourself.

📍 3229 NW Pittock Dr, Portland West Hills
💰 $5 adults | Grounds accessible before mansion opens

Most people visit on a cloudy afternoon and wonder what the fuss is about. Go early on a clear morning and look east. The view is a genuine argument for living here.

Learn More

Cascada Thermal Springs

Indoor and outdoor soaking pools, saunas, and Forbidden Fruit restaurant on-site. The do-nothing, say-nothing, just-relax version of Mothers Day.

📍 1150 NE Alberta St, Portland
💰 Ticketed | Reserve now — this one fills up

Not every Mom wants a hike or a brunch. Some want to sit in hot water and not be asked a single question. This is that option, and it is a very good one.

Learn More

Leach Botanical Garden with Celtic Harp

Most Portlanders have never been here. On Mothers Day there's live music and refreshments in a historic manor house — one of the best things happening this Sunday.

📍 6704 SE 122nd Ave, Portland Lents neighborhood
📅 Sunday May 10

Out in the Lents neighborhood where most visitors never venture. The garden makes you slow down without trying. Worth building a whole Mothers Day around this one and very few people will have the same idea.

Learn More

Champagne Poetry

Souffle pancakes that take 30 minutes and are worth every minute. Playful, pink, and a local find most visitors never discover.

📍 1620 NW 23rd Ave, Portland
💰 Regular menu pricing

NW 23rd is one of Portland's most walkable neighborhood streets. Champagne Poetry feels like a discovery even if you've lived here for years. The souffle pancakes alone are worth the trip.

Learn More

The Mothers Day Table at Cooper Mountain Vineyards

Sparkling pours, white wines, and spring pairings under the oaks in Beaverton — quieter and more grown-up than the larger vineyard events, closer to Portland than most people expect.

📅 Sunday May 10
📍 Cooper Mountain Vineyards, Beaverton | 💰 Ticketed

Cooper Mountain is one of those Oregon wineries that rewards the people who seek it out. Closer than most wine country options and genuinely unhurried.
